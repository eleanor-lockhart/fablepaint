# Concept Film Brief — *Shuttered Lamp* (`kaledathela_concept.mp4`)

- **Purpose:** 32.0-second (`768` frames at `24 fps`, `16` bars of `4/4` at `120 BPM`) visual/thematic setting test for **Kaledathela**. No lore exposition—every rule of the world (the Primary and Worm Subspace Border, West Texas caprock vs. East Texas bayou, four ruin strata, The Pastor's technological Inquisition, and the Coven's light-stealth survival around `0xF` and `0x5..0x7`) is shown through staging, colour registers, and sound sync.
- **Resolution & Finish:** `1280 × 720`, indexed `640 × 360` world plates blitted `2×` with crisp `8×8` ROM HUD overlays (`ui.py`) and `ui.glow` strictly restricted to **register `F` (`#ffd870`)**.
