# What was looked at

References are for looking and measuring. Neither picture is in this repository, and nothing from them is in
the film except numbers read off the first.

- Eadweard Muybridge, *The Horse in Motion*: "Sallie Gardner," owned by Leland Stanford, running at a 1:40 gait
  over the Palo Alto track, 19 June 1878. Public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Horse_in_Motion_high_res.jpg
  Frames 2 to 11 were enlarged under a grid and the slope of each leg bone read off against the vertical. The
  table of those slopes is at the top of `horse.py`.
- Eadweard Muybridge, *Animal Locomotion*, plate 626 (1887), a horse galloping in sixteen frames. Public domain.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Animal_locomotion._Plate_626_(Boston_Public_Library).jpg
  Looked at for the order of the footfalls. No numbers taken.

What a set of gallopers is (a platform hung from a centre pole, horses on cranked poles that rise and fall,
rounding boards, a band organ alongside, the whole thing wintered in a shed) is general knowledge, and the ride
here is not a copy of any particular one. No real ride turns 120 times a minute.
