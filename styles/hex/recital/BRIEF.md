# SPACE OPERA (IN ONE ACT): the plan

The second piece in the Hex style. Ninety-six seconds, 1280 x 720, 24 frames a second. The song is the piece.

**One sentence.** Aboard the research vessel *Perimeter* the witch conducts a space opera, which is an opera, in
space; everyone you hear is someone you can see, and she does not allow applause.

**How it is made.** `music.py` writes the song and, with it, `events.json`: every note, when, how long and by
whom. `film.py` draws from that list and nothing else, so a player moves when, and only when, they play.

**Two eras on one screen.**

| from before 2000 | from now |
|---|---|
| the band, as sprites, moving on twos | ribbons in a flow field behind them, on ones, coloured by the chord |
| a tracker: the song scrolling past as rows of notes and hex | the whole melody drawn as one line across the sky, so that by the end the song is a skyline |
| a player window with a scrolling title, a clock and sixteen bars | a rose window whose twelve lights are the twelve pitch classes |
| a dialog box with three buttons | a glow, and only register F has it |

**Who plays what.** The spider: its web, which is a harp. The rats, in the pit: the strings, bowing together.
The druid: the whistle. An acolyte: the drums. The cowboy: the fiddle, once he commits. A newt: the bass. The
faculty: the organ. The drones: the choir. The bloc: the guitars. The witch: nothing. She conducts, with her
back to us, by one small lamp in register F, and she is the only thing on the stage that is never lit.

**The form** (a bar is two seconds): the baton, four times; the spider and the rats (bars 1-4); a verse (5-12);
organ and choir closing in (13-16); the chorus in D minor (17-24); the organ alone (25-28); a short verse
(29-32); the chorus a tone higher (33-40); three chords and a major one, cut off on the third beat (41-44);
then nothing. A dialog asks: APPLAUSE? [ALLOW] [DENY] [NEWTS]. She presses DENY. The spider plays one more note
anyway.

**What it must say to a stranger:** each instrument belongs to a creature; the figure in the hat is in charge
and makes no sound; the music gets bigger as more of them light up; it stops when she says; she refuses the
applause; something small has the last word.
